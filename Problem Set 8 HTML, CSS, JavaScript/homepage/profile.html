I was born in Mollerussa, where I spent most of my childhood and teenage years. Since I was a kid, I’ve always been a curious person, eager to understand how things work. I studied at El Carme and later at Claver, and I’ve always been drawn to two things: music and mathematics. I’ve been playing the guitar since I was little, and over time it became a way to disconnect and express myself.

Sports have also been a big part of my life — I played tennis and did horse riding when I was young, and later I got into skiing and hiking, which I really enjoy today. I’ve also been lucky to travel quite a lot, which opened my mind and made me appreciate learning about other cultures and ways of thinking.

When it came time to choose what to study, I decided to pursue a Bachelor’s Degree in Telecommunications Engineering (specializing in Audiovisual Systems) at Pompeu Fabra University, because I thought it was the right balance between the technical and the creative sides. Over time, I realized what truly interests me is technology as a tool to solve real problems and to understand the world better.

During my summers at university, I worked at Nufri SAT 1596, collaborating with the CTO of the Nufri Group, where I got to see how technology is managed inside a large company. Later, I did my university internship at Terlas Software, developing an industrial project using Python and Arduino for quality control in pharmaceutical processes.

After those experiences, I wanted to understand how a small company works from the inside, to see what it takes to start and grow a business. That’s why I joined Wasabi Zebra, where I worked until October 2025 in a cross-functional role involving Finance, Sales, and Operations.

I’ve completed a Master’s Degree in Industrial Business Management (IQS) and I’m currently pursuing a Master’s in Telecommunications Engineering (UOC). Over time, my interests have expanded toward entrepreneurship, programming, and finance — fields that connect analytical thinking with creativity.
