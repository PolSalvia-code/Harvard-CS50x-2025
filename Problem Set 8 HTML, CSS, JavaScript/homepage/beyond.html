I don’t really know what the meaning of all this is, but I do know what I’d like: to spend the time I have here learning, doing things well, and enjoying the process.

Curiosity is what drives me. I’ve always wanted to understand why things happen — how a system works, how people think, or how ideas connect. When you understand a bit more about what surrounds you, everything seems to make a bit more sense.

I’d also like to make a living doing something that inspires me, and if possible, something that adds value to others. Not to play the hero, but because when you do something that helps or has an impact, it’s better for everyone — and for yourself too.

I try to live simply: to understand, to learn, and to do things properly. Spending time with the people I care about, playing guitar, going to the mountains, or just disconnecting.

In the end, I guess it all comes down to this: following curiosity, living with purpose, and leaving something good behind.
